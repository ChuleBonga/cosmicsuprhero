## Blocking issues

1. **CRITICAL / W0 visibility** — At the inhabited shelter the figure reads as a low-contrast blue mannequin rather than an actual person. The head/eyes/facial structure, voluminous garment and expressive behavior are not legible. Improve actual sculpt, controlled character key light, silhouette/stage position and readable animation before adding more NPCs.
2. **HIGH / terrain materials** — Existing albedo/vertex-color multiplication yields orange-brown ground despite the album's blue geology. Rebuild the terrain shading from physically plausible rock reference; use coherent UV scale, roughness and localized ochre rather than stretched flickering noise.
3. **HIGH / sun and shadows** — Current red disc is flat/soft and disconnected from overly orange ground and black foregrounds; lock a physically coherent key direction, allow realistic fill and contact AO rather than flattening ambient or arbitrary glow.
4. **HIGH / waystation architecture** — Shelter is assembled from procedural rectangular bars; redesign with actual structure (load paths, joints, attachments, workshop tools, use-wear, cloth behavior) and environmental context.
5. **HIGH / inhabited world** — No houses, lived-in district, visible people, transport or relationship ecosystem behind the route. An isolated NPC by a bench is not a civilization.

## Separate technical facts

A previous browser gameplay test loaded W0's original Blender GLB (41 meshes, three clips, 1.839 m height, zero reported ground clearance), verified natural walking and an E acknowledgement, and found no severe console error in that run. Normal `/play/` did not load W0. Another critical browser audit also successfully rendered W0 but reported *mobile-emulated touch controls hidden*, as well as a later asset URL that returned HTML rather than JS, indicating a possible stale-route/deployment cache mismatch. These contradictory observations require dedicated current-version verification; do not declare mobile gameplay fully passed on evidence from a single emulator. Native smartphone performance is **not measured**.

## Quality gate and work order

**Do not turn this failed look into the normal visitor experience.** Current `look=cover` remains opt-in through a named review pose, and W0 is visible only via `?art-review=inhabitants`.

A. Fix ground shader/albedo and disable unapproved sun-obscuring geometry **in the review look only**. Capture exactly matched before/after in-browser frames and compare to approved album art. A code change alone is not an art pass.

B. Produce a reference-specific architectural and inhabited-location design (real street, dwellings, a credible workshop with true load-bearing structure and evidence of people), then original model assets. Avoid generic ornament.

C. Fix W0 sculpt/silhouette, visible eyes, face, garments, workbench alignment and hand/probe contact. Use actual Blender mesh/animation and visually test action at real in-game scale, not a static high-quality illustration.

D. Implement a second NPC with an independent role/relationship once W0 and the world context pass stage G1. Approve character/story canon before names or dramatic dialogue.

E. Finish real desktop+mobile inspection, recovery, video/voice rights, actual GPU frame-pacing and restore checks. Confirm cached asset behavior before promoting.

## Acceptance standard

**G1 visual direction and G2 production art are BLOCKED.** A passing WebGL render, functional keyboard interaction, successful Three.js parser test or Vercel READY are necessary engineering evidence but **not artistic acceptance**. All public album/pre-save routes stay intact. Only the artist can approve new definitive visual canon.

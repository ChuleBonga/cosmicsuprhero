# Arcturian Awakening / complete working asset register

This is a **living, scoped production inventory**, not a claim that every later chapter asset is already knowable. It covers a first inhabited playable location (P0), candidate expansion (P1), and promotional outputs. New scenes must append specific character performance, props, environments, voice and effects before implementation.

CSV source: `ASSET_REGISTER.csv` contains **120 distinct deliverables across 11 workstreams**. Quantity values indicate expected variants or clips; they are not counts of accepted assets. The status `existing-prototype/rework` indicates a functioning baseline requiring professional art QA, not a completed asset.

## Priorities
- **P0** = needed for a believable inhabited first playable location, including its quality and marketing prerequisites.
- **P1** = expanded neighborhood population, scope and richer social/musical systems.
- **P2** = later world regions and album-scale chapters; append once canonical narrative is settled.

## Required states
`planned → concept-only → artist-approved → built → engine-integrated → tested → released`.
Also use `blocked-by-rights` or `rejected`. Every deliverable tracks original source/reference, expected format, quantity, dependencies, acceptance and ownership.

**First art targets:** REF-001 to REF-008 and CHAR-001/CHAR-005; then ENV-006 and PROP-001/PROP-002. Do not sculpt generic architecture before the reference/frame and character identity locks are accepted.

Concept image ≠ exportable model. Built model ≠ integrated scene. Integrated scene ≠ approved experience.

# PROP-001 · FIRST CONTACT PORTABLE RECORDER · R0
**Status: CONCEPT-ONLY / REJECT UNTIL ART AND STORY REVIEW** · 2026-11-11

## What actually exists
- [Interactive Blender-authored 3D prototype in Higgsfield 3D Jutsu](https://higgsfield.ai/3d-jutsu/0eda9e71-40bf-4fcc-b651-7f9ad7e507d5), revision 2.
- Original hard-surface design authored in metres. Approximate width **0.274 m**, length **0.185 m**, shell height **0.084 m** (small mechanical protuberances may exceed nominal shell dimensions).
- Scene audit: **80 mesh objects**, each with assigned material; **2 distinct mechanical reels**, **5 physical action keys**, an analog adjustment group, carry loop / burgundy fabric band; **2,502 polygons** across the whole scene including the presentation floor. This is a size/structure audit, **not visual approval**.
- Material roles: oxidized cobalt steel, dark recesses, copper-ochre worn metal, gray steel, burgundy cloth, magnetic tape and black keys. The detailed render was created in the hosted authoring project, but the generated preview has **not been independently visually accepted**.
- The `STUDIO_ground` object is an authoring backdrop and **must be excluded** in a production GLB export. Convert the model to a clean single-root game asset, reduce material calls, check UVs/normals, pivots, orientation and collision before integration.

## Why this belongs to the album world (candidate, not established canon)
- A **physical device for preserving and replaying recorded human speech and music**, with functional reels and controls instead of an arbitrary magical collectible.
- The recording's actual author, history, contents, ownership, relationship to Chule, relevance of Credo, and effect on the civilization are **not approved** and will not be invented as facts.
- No third-party product logo or sacred Xhosa/Kemetic symbol is present. Artist-supplied 43-board device images informed category only; their exact designs were not imported or copied.
- The existing physical cobalt relic remains in the live game. **Do not silently substitute this candidate for it** until the artist accepts its design and story function.

## Decision
**R0 is an exploration asset, not game-ready.** It is not used in the public build. Current user's main priority remains a visually convincing inhabited world with purposeful NPCs and objects.

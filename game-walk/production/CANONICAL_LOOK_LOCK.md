# Canonical artwork colour and composition lock
Status: **measured visual reference; game fidelity NOT approved** · 2026-11-11

I recovered the original 3000×3000 image embedded on page 1 of the user-supplied `Arcturian Awakening — Cosmicsuprhero.pdf` in the project conversation. The album cover includes a central illustrated planet inset, a gold horizontal graphic overlay across the portrait, white page margins and typography. **Do not treat the white borders or band as 3D landscape details.**

Measured sRGB reference tokens are in [CANONICAL_PALETTE.json](CANONICAL_PALETTE.json). Crop in original image roughly x950–2048, y535–2165. Color sampling uses local patch medians to reduce the deliberate artwork grain and is **not** exact shader input or a license to recolor the world without GPU screenshot comparison.

## What's actually in the image (not invented moodboard lore)
- A dense **cobalt/indigo sky** carrying one large, clearly edged, saturated **red-orange circular sun**.
- Layered pointed **deep-blue mountains**, with strong coarse **golden ochre highlights** that define ridges and sediment contours. Mountains dominate the world, not stone palaces.
- Lower terrain continues these blue/ochre textures.
- One bald, elongated-headed **mottled blue-green humanoid** with a subtly lined face, dark eyes, expressive side-glance and voluminous **burgundy / deep-purple hooded garment**.
- Granular analog illustration/print texture. Real-world scene interpretations may be cinematic but must retain this unmistakable identity.

## What repeated generated concepts have gotten wrong
- Defaulting to polished, grandiose **golden city / temple / market** imagery with generic ornate sci-fi.
- Assuming Xhosa/Kemetic motifs or cultural history belong together because both appear in the private reference board.
- Inventing named family ties, NPC appearances, runic symbols, patterned costumes and glowing relays without artist approval.
- Interpreting absence of people in the album crop as license for an empty uninhabited game.

## First three required original scene keyframes
1. **Arrival:** low eye height, physically convincing road and distant occupied landscape, dramatic album blue geological silhouette and giant **sharp-edged orange-red sun**. No generic yellow sky, gigantic new arch or fantasy trees.
2. **Everyday location:** a modest inhabited space on that planet with ordinary use and meaningful building functions; use the private 43-image board as research **after exact images are available**. Reject copied motifs and generic North African / golden-city approximations.
3. **First Contact:** protagonist or another approved inhabitant interacts with a mechanical sound object of understood purpose. The person, artifact owner and story consequence are **unapproved**, so the shot must be treated as exploratory until character canon is signed off.

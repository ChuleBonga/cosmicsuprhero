# World Focus 01: a trace of everyday life

The live Arcturian world had a strong sky and causeway but repeated monumental gateways, generic floating shards, and no ordinary architecture. This narrow art pass replaces the second duplicate arch with a roadside listening/repair shelter and removes the floating markers.

The shelter includes a damaged canopy, workbench, a nonfunctional original listening shell, cloth, vessels and repairs to the stone roadway. It is evidence of use and material culture, **not** an established historical settlement or a named character's home. It is not interactive yet. No copied cultural symbols or mood-board files are introduced.

The story still needs real people, relationships, work, social conflict and more than one location. This scenic addition does not claim to solve that. The first-person camera, authentic First Contact relic, end screen and pre-save are preserved.

Quality gates: match visible footprint to physics collider, test forward passage, verify browser scene and player controls, verify responsive view and frame pacing. A Vercel build alone does not prove cinematic art quality.

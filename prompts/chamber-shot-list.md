## Crop Guidance

- Hero/section plates: 16:9 master, 1920px wide minimum web export.
- Mobile-safe crop: keep primary subject or light source near the center third, with clean negative space above and below.
- Card crops: avoid important faces, hands, text-like artifacts, and fine symbolic details near the edges.
- Full chamber backgrounds: keep contrast low enough for white/cream text over a dark veil.

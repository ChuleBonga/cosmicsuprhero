# Cosmicsuprhero Visual Direction

The site is not a normal music portfolio. It is a living sonic observatory where each release becomes a chamber and the whole experience behaves like a continuous cinematic transmission.

## Core Visual Language

- Sacred brutalist architecture
- Concrete monoliths
- Impossible stairways
- Analog signal equipment
- South African memory
- Township road textures
- Dust, sunlight, taxis, walking, masks, speakers
- Cinematic documentary realism
- Warm human foregrounds
- Cold monumental background spaces
- Alien tenderness without horror
- Cosmic scale without Marvel-style spectacle

## Design Rule

Human documentary realism in the foreground.

Impossible sacred architecture in the background.

Cosmicsuprhero should feel real, tactile, human, and emotionally grounded. The world around him can feel ancient, impossible, cosmic, brutalist, and symbolic.

## Avoid

- Generic sci-fi
- Cyberpunk cliches
- Superhero aesthetics
- Glossy AI fantasy
- Neon overload
- Marvel armor
- Fake Afrofuturist tropes
- Corporate portfolio styling
- Luxury showroom emptiness
- Generic galaxy wallpaper

## Chamber Visual Rules

### Observatory

Brutalist cosmic archive, analog consoles, amber instruments, concrete silence, celestial maps, sacred bunker atmosphere, warm signal equipment inside cold monumental space.

### Soil and Sun

South African road memory, golden sun, dust, taxis, walking, speakers, masks, township texture, concrete walls, childhood, prayer, movement, ordinary life made mythic.

### Songs I Made At Night

Blue-black nocturnal room, laptop glow, cassette archive, window reflections, migration memory, emotional stillness, private thought.

### Bad Guys Finish Last

Wet concrete, red-black night, tunnel or underpass atmosphere, cracked surfaces, rain particles, urban ruin, emotional consequence, shadow without losing humanity.

### Enter Aquarius

Impossible concrete stairways, blue-gray brutalist threshold, celestial weather, storm pressure, ascension, the body crossing into another frequency.

### Celestial Multiverse

Monolithic archive structures, star maps, floating artifacts, cosmic expansion, alien artifacts, inherited transmissions, vast scale with human memory still present.

### Stargazing, Vol. 3

Suspended intimate star field, quiet observation, blue-black sky, distant lights, human loneliness under cosmic scale, peaceful cosmic intimacy.

### Just Sounds Really

Sparse sound object, minimal room, residue, restraint, quiet signal lines, tactile blank space, almost empty but intentional.

### Arcturian Awakening

Brutalist temple, alien softness, violet and gold signal light, concrete and stars, sacred awakening, human body inside impossible scale, ancient childlike intelligence.

## Alien Tenderness

The Arcturian side should feel strange but innocent:

- ancient childlike intelligence
- quiet observer
- soft otherworldly presence
- cosmic innocence
- symbolic background figures, statues, mural faces, masks, or chamber artifacts

Do not turn Cosmicsuprhero into an alien figure. The human remains human. The alien energy surrounds the archive.

## World-Plate Batch

Generate world plates before portraits or character scenes.

Important generation rule:

```text
Create ONE standalone cinematic image only.
No collage.
No grid.
No split panels.
No text.
No labels.
No captions.
No watermark.
This is a single website background asset.
```

Do not ask for an "asset set" in one image generation. The model may interpret that as a contact sheet or moodboard. Generate one final asset per generation, save it with the correct filename, then move to the next asset.

Priority world plates and filenames:

1. Brutalist observatory interior
2. Impossible concrete stairway threshold
3. South African road memory with dust and taxis
4. Warm township speaker / sound-system artifact
5. Concrete monolith observatory exterior
6. Blue nocturnal archive room
7. Red-black wet concrete shadow chamber
8. Alien artifact portrait / Arcturian relic
9. Celestial archive tower
10. Sparse sound-object room

```text
01_observatory_interior.webp
02_impossible_stairway.webp
03_soil_and_sun_road.webp
04_sound_system_speaker.webp
05_observatory_exterior.webp

```

Workflow:

```text
world first
character second
website integration third
```

## Generation Quality Bar

All images should feel:

- camera-real
- cinematic
- tactile
- emotionally human
- grounded in documentary texture
- expanded by mythic architecture

The goal is not "cool sci-fi." The goal is a sacred archive where human memory is held inside impossible architecture.

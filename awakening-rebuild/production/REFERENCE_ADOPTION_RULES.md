## Most important translation rules
- The **album cover**, not the other concept images, defines sky/sun/geology/species/hooded-cloth core identity.
- Blue-faced concept portraits can inform face microtexture or acting, but must not overwrite the original head/eye proportions.
- Architecture inspirations show ways buildings relate to people, transport, work and public life; do not fill the world with repeated generic towers and arches.
- Analog consumer electronics imply tactile controls and service history. Create an original communication technology and record why it exists in the society; do not reproduce branded consumer products.
- Textiles and symbolic items must be motivated by culture and role; motifs associated with real peoples or faiths require sensitive attribution/approval, not arbitrary game ornament.
- Landscape reference is not authority to turn the original cobalt-orange setting into green Earth or a generic brown desert.
- A repeating mountain image with two suns must not be tiled to simulate a single-sun sky.

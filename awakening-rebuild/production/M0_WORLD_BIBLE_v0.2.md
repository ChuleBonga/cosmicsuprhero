# Arrival Quarter: coherent civilization grammar v0.2
**Proposed world design, not approved final art.** Reviewed source: artist's album illustration, 43-image `more.zip`, `QUALITY_CONTRACT.md`, `CHR-002_CARETAKER_ASSET_SPEC.md`, and current source simulation. Maintain the original art as the visual authority.

## Distinct visual grammar
**Landscape and exposure:** a single orange-red sun against cobalt/indigo atmosphere; dark-blue mountainous geology with controlled warm ochre edge illumination. Foreground work must remain readable without replacing the blue world with beige or plastic orange spill. Do not tile `aa_mountain_repeat.webp` as sky: it contains duplicated suns.

**The civilization must be inhabited:** a smaller working/domestic district feeds one more prominent shared hall. A courier handoff and a maintenance/workshop activity explain why this neighborhood exists. Residents carry out different work even while the player is silent. Public spaces permit gathering and disagreement; households protect private history.

**Architecture hypothesis (requires original elevations before approval):** human-scale stone/mineral ground contacts, weathered blue-gray infill, sheltered repairable structures, restrained layered fabric for shade, service channels accessible to workers, and clear door/sill/roof construction logic. No repeated generic arches, copied spaceship rail station, empty temple nave or enormous monolith as substitute for a town. Inspiration: R06/R07/R11/R16/R22/R26/R28/R42. These materials and forms are *proposals*; the references do not prove local construction methods.

**Wardrobe and anatomy:** preserve the original illustration's elongated head, recessed dark eyes and blue skin; don't replace it with the embellished identities in R01–R03. Favor tactile, heavy burgundy-purple everyday textiles with functional closure, repairs and gravity. Movement needs realistic garment accommodation. R19/R20/R43 are culturally specific: don't reuse patterns by default. There is no approved 360-degree caretaker anatomy yet.

**Technology:** interpret R05/R08/R12/R13/R31/R32/R35/R40/R41 as evidence for physical media, hand-operated controls, repair, storage and public service, not as permission to introduce branded terrestrial devices. Before revising civic receiver PRP-001, answer who operates it, what travels through it, how it fails, who maintains it and why community members care.

**Evidence objects:** household ledger at (4.4,5.2), courier case near (22,5.6), receiver at (15,7.5) are from `DistrictSimulation.ts`; these are work objects with custody and consequences, not collectible items.

## Acceptance: actual world, not a mood board
- One distinct resident must be sculpted and approved in neutral and album light, then rigged for idle/work, locomotion, listening, speaking, trust/refusal and return to work; actual medium-shot acting test at 390px and 1080p.
- A 40-second *uncontrolled* shot shows at least three resident tasks and one social exchange independent of player input.
- A player can move through a sensible public-to-private spatial transition; every important object has owner, physical handling, maintenance story and usable pivot.
- Two moral outcomes change resident movement/availability and access to a *playable* place, not just text.
- Compare image and actual 3D capture to original album source; artist accepts architecture and culture. No generic alien city aesthetic accepted.

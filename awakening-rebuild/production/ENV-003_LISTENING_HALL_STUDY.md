# ENV-003/004 — Working Listening Hall: review handoff, 18 November 2026

**Disposition:** FUNCTIONAL MASSING STUDY, not artist-approved environment art, not player-facing. A modeled public-space volume and working-object use case to test the source-grounded M0 architecture hypothesis.

## Why it exists
The hall is a place for shared listening, records and interpersonal disagreement. Its physical plan distinguishes public access from household confidentiality. It includes benches for ordinary visitors, service equipment accessible to the apprentice and a handoff ledge used by a courier. No giant shrine, arbitrary arch, magic portal, neon ring or imported historical motifs.

## Exact geometric commitments (study, not building-code approval)
- Public room footprint 11.2 × 8.4 metres, nominal top of roof about 3.92 m from grade
- Entry void 2.6 m wide and nominally 3.0 m high, near-flush door sill
- Gradual approach ramp ≈2.78 m long; slope needs later accessibility and collider verification
- Six meeting benches in opposed groupings, 0.46 m seat surface
- External service hatch center 1.15 m from ground; manual fasteners and cloth-wrapped contact
- Roof 12.0 m wide, 10.6 m front-to-back, with ≈1.5 m front shade and measurable runoff path
- Upper clerestory openings are actual wall gaps, not simulated dark vent stickers
- Replaceable burgundy acoustic baffles are attached to frames
- Original 85-mesh watertight GLB, 1,020 triangles with individually named, purpose-driven parts; original material colors only, not authored texture maps

## Review criteria
**Technical:** glTF 2.0 import, named parts, valid bounds, positive/watertight geometries, no blocked 2.6 m doorway, controller/collision tests once integrated, 390px/1080p render captures.
**Art:** reject as visually complete if it still appears to be a generic roof-on-box construction. Add an approved local construction grammar, tectonic detail, material wear, hand-built hierarchy and a socially active scene before promoting.
**Gameplay:** caretaker/apprentice/courier physical routines, entry/exit and choice-dependent accessibility must be implemented, not inferred from this model.
**Rights:** author-owned geometry; reference illustrations are research, not direct republished texture assets.

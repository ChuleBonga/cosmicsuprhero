# The Return: opening chapter beat board v0.2
**Provisional 12–18 minute playable chapter, not album canon.** Derived from `encounter.ts`, `aftermath.ts`, `DistrictSimulation.ts` and project story intentions. The current reducer says dispatch precedes refuge, which precedes the hall's silence. It does NOT establish the final cause of the event, Credo's relationship or the player's reason to return. Those remain subject to artist decision.

**Narrative hypothesis (to approve or replace):** an incomplete family-linked recording draws Chule to the Arrival Quarter; a disruption to public communications left a disputed history. Publishing the message can support accountability but endangers households' privacy. These are two legitimate interests. Credo holds context still needed; their actual role has not been defined.

**Player test:** By minute five, player explains why Chule came, why community is affected and what they can do. By end, points to a world-visible changed routine/location, not just new prose.

**Character motive proposal:** caretaker protects confidences; courier wants accountable chain of custody; apprentice wants reliable public access. The reader should not automatically treat any of these as antagonist.

**Artist decisions:** Chule's true return motive; Credo's real role; whether the record dispute is suitable; why release/withhold matters to family; authorized audio, voice and cut points. No generated fictional dialogue/audio is approved.

**Implementation:** reuse deterministic pure narrative state, but replace its card UI with physically triggered observations, staged dialogue and persistent animated NPC state.

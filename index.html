02 / The passage

THREE MOVEMENTS.

A musical story that moves outwards, bends toward personal history, and finds its way into the present.

I / 03

THE ENCOUNTER

Stillness first. Then an unfamiliar sense of proximity. Meditate and First Contact introduce the unknown without asking the listener to fear it.

II / 03

THE HUMAN WEIGHT

Distance gives way to names, relationships, and earthly questions. This is where the record becomes most personal, and where the sky is no longer enough.

III / 03

THE RETURN

Rhythm and movement come back. The question changes from where we might travel to how we inhabit the life already in our hands.

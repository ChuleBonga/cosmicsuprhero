Behind the record

Some thoughts
just arrive.

Imagine sitting quietly and watching a thought appear without chasing it. Maybe it is a memory. Maybe it is fear, or something that makes you smile. Sometimes you cannot tell where it came from.

Then imagine meeting something beyond the noise. A sense of light, a connection, a sudden understanding you did not know you were carrying.

The landscape is imagined. The human experiences behind these songs are not.

# Cosmicsuprhero Identity Lock

Use the character reference sheet in `source/reference/cosmicsuprhero-character-reference-sheet.jpeg` as a supporting source, not as the only truth. Original real photos should remain the primary likeness source for future image generation.

Preserve:
- rounded-square face
- soft jawline, not a sharpened model jaw
- compact chin
- warm brown eyes
- slightly heavy eyelids
- medium-width rounded nose
- expressive lips
- soft mustache
- light natural beard/goatee
- medium-dark warm brown skin
- natural skin texture
- grounded contemplative expression
- human vulnerability and emotional gravity

Hair states:
- Observatory/formal/Soil and Sun: shaped afro or natural controlled silhouette
- Cosmic drift/nocturnal/awakening: natural locs/freeform twists with asymmetry

Avoid:
- generic Black male protagonist
- superhero styling
- idealized symmetrical face
- sharpened jawline
- altered eyes, nose, lips, or facial hair
- plastic AI skin
- fantasy armor
- cyberpunk clichés
- glowing eyes
- overdramatic poster lighting
- making the character look like an actor/model instead of the real artist

Creative rule:
Human first. Cosmic second. The world may be immense, but the face must remain emotionally recognizable and grounded.

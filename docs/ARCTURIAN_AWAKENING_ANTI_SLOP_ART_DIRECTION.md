# Arcturian Awakening — creative direction & anti-slop standard
**Artist instruction:** 2026-11-24. **Applies to:** all future social carousels, release posters, website promotional graphics and related Arcturian Awakening visual campaigns. **Status:** standing artist quality requirement, not an approved finished design.

## Central creative idea
Arcturian Awakening is a *meditative sonic landscape* about becoming still enough to observe thoughts. Some feel like memories, fears or joy; others seem to arrive from the ether. In this state a person may experience an unexpected "download" or connection with a source of light already within us, and emerge changed. Communicate this as an imaginative/spiritual experience, not a scientific claim or scripted outcome for every listener. Make room for the audience's interpretation. Music and game are related artistic worlds but **the game does not have to follow the album's sixteen tracks**.

## Source-of-truth visual identity
The **original approved album artwork** governs the world: cobalt/indigo sky, one immense saturated orange-red sun, dark-blue mountains with restrained ochre light, a distinctive blue-skinned humanoid with elongated head and dark eyes, and a heavily weathered burgundy-purple hood/cloak. Do not drift into generic aliens, altered facial identity, beige deserts, replacement yellow suns, or royalty-like ornamental costumes. Review new compositions alongside the original; do not silently treat generated re-imaginings as official artwork.

## Specific failure patterns to reject
1. AI-looking cream/beige + giant luxury serif + orange accent + thin dividers + gratuitous star/orbit decorations repeated as a ready-made template.
2. Repeating the **same crop/portrait** across most slides, using changed words to simulate variety. Six images should have six meaningful artistic reasons to exist.
3. Predictable, interchangeable card layouts, excessive symmetrical balance, forced faux-vintage grain, arbitrary worn paper textures, purposeless glow, grids, circles and astronomical glyphs.
4. Generic inspirational marketing and formulaic AI-copy cadences: "Not X. Y.", abstract trios, "journey of...", vague "music, memory, belonging" declarations. Prefer plain and sometimes surprising language rooted in the artist's thoughts.
5. Letting an image generator render all finished poster text. Compose/type-set separately to control exact spelling, kerning, line breaks, contrast, baseline alignment, safe areas and release metadata.
6. Impossibly pristine/staged AI visual theater. Also **do not simulate human authorship** by introducing arbitrary mistakes or distressed textures.
7. Treating a successful export or correct social aspect ratio as evidence of accomplished art direction.

## Direction
- **Cinematic:** each visual is a distinct moment from this coherent imagined world; original perspective, believable material and grounded light.
- **Intimate:** human specificity, memory, contemplation, family, faith, hesitation and wonder rather than impersonal campaign language.
- **Beautifully unsettling:** meditative stillness; something unexpected enters consciousness; a connection to light; a change of perception.
- Keep the identity consistent across a carousel **without making every slide identical**. Variations should come from story, camera distance, environment, point of view, symbol or pacing.
- Copy should be natural, particular, and short enough for social viewing; it must not explain away all of the mystery.
- Treat the album announcement as distinct from the narrative carousel; each post must be legible and understandable on its own.

## Mandatory production workflow
1. Define the idea and purpose **for each slide**, then sketch the carousel's narrative arc and the single-image reading.
2. Establish a small art-direction system with exact colors from the approved artwork, type hierarchy, margins, contrast and image-treatment rules.
3. Produce or source artwork **separately** from final layout. Protect distinctive character identity and scene details. AI-generated imagery is a proposed *source asset*, not automatically final art. Check source rights.
4. Design typography and composition with precision in an editable tool; export final **standalone images** in the requested aspect ratio and pixel dimensions.
5. Review **each image individually at phone scale**, then all images side by side: readability, spelling, artist name, track names, release date, consistency, visual variety, meaningful negative space, safe zones and integrity to canon.
6. Remove typography and inspect the visual storytelling: does each slide still say something distinctive? If five images are interchangeable, reject and revise.
7. Perform an explicit **anti-slop red-team pass** before sharing. Check for fake stars, rings, tiny uppercase labels, generic beige/serif prestige styling, decoration for its own sake, overprocessed textures, AI artifacts, malformed words, and recycled crops.
8. Never call the output "finished", "approved" or "ready to post" until these visual gates pass and the artist has reviewed it. Share what was verified vs. what remains subjective or unverified.

## Last campaign review outcome
The initial 6-slide two-carousel campaign was rejected as creative underdevelopment. It reused the same main art, made the album's awakening concept too vague, regressed to stock press-copy, and repeated nearly identical ivory/burgundy editorial layouts. Some previous image-model generations altered the figure's appearance. The carousel export size was 1080×1350 portrait despite an initial promise of square. Preserve the lesson; do not reuse those assets as approved templates.

## Practical acceptance criterion
**Could this only belong to Cosmicsuprhero's Arcturian Awakening?** If replacing the artist name and cover would make it a plausible poster for dozens of other releases, the design is not specific enough. The goal is not to *hide AI involvement* but to make intentional, rigorous work whose subject, content, layout and craft genuinely belong to this record.
